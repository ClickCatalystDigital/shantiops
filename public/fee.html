A proposal for Phase 1: what's already running, what finishing it looks like, and what it costs.

What's already running

This wasn't built as a generic tracker with your logo on it. It was built around your actual shop floor — the milestone names match what your team already calls things, and the packing list PDF matches the paperwork you already use.

Operations platform

Design to dispatch, one system

Tracks every order from design through commissioning — milestones, the Bill of Materials, packing lists, and a live status page your customers can check themselves.

Security platform

Every machine, protected

Locks down USB drives, CDs, phones, and websites on factory computers until a manager approves — with a full record of every approval.

Management Operation System

Approvals & department handoffs, coordinated

A coordination layer sitting across both platforms — who's approved, and who picks up the baton when one department finishes and the next one starts. Broken out in the Investment section below.

What finishes Phase 1

Phase 1 is complete once every department head and project manager is actually using the system day to day — logged in, machine set up, working from it instead of the old process. Guided onboarding for every department is included at no extra cost, not billed separately. Alongside that, Phase 1 includes a tailored market research report on your cloud storage options, based on the questionnaire your team already completed.
