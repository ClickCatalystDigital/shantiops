## Text that changes inside the forms (what the 2026 version of each page prints)
**Form I (Part-B)** (was II(1)): heading `FORM-I (PART-B) [See regulation 8(a)] CERTIFICATE OF INSPECTION FOR SHOP ASSEMBLED
BOILERS`; "regulations framed under the Boilers Act, 2025" (was 1923); stamp block adds `Design Pressure of boiler`; closing
sentence cites `Form-IV(Part-A)` (was Form III). Signatures: Competent Person / Inspecting Authority, Date and Seal (unchanged).

**Form IV (Part-A)** (was III): `[See regulation 8(b)]`. §1 adds `Design pressure of boiler` and `Reheater Pressure`, `Diameter
inside Largest belt`, `Evaporation capacity`. §4: "in accordance with Chapter 3/4/10/12/14/16/17 of the Indian Boiler Regulations"
(was III/V/X/XII/XIV); seams per belt / per ring unchanged; adds `Details of heat treatment`. §5 Drums/Shells and §6 Headers and
Boxes keep the same columns. §7 Mountings unchanged list. §8 heading `{Regulation 8(d)(vii)}` (was 4(c)(vii)). §9: "comply with the
regulations under the Boilers Act, 2025, for a Design pressure of" (was "Indian Boilers Act, 1923 for a Working Pressure of").
Sign-off: Maker's Representative / Maker, then Competent Person / Inspecting Authority, "Dated … the day of … 20…".

**Form IV (Part-B)** (was III A): `[See regulation 10] CERTIFICATE OF MANUFACTURE AND TEST FOR PIPES`; "constructed in accordance
with Indian Boiler Regulations 2026"; Note 3: one form for not more than five pipes (stock and sale).

**Form IV (Part-G)** (was III-H): `[See regulation 15]`; "constructed to comply with the Indian Boiler Regulations, 2026"; raw
material reference is "Form-V(Part-B)" (was Form IV-A).

**Form V (Part-B)** (was IV A): `[Regulation 8(d)] CERTIFICATE OF MANUFACTURE AND RESULTS OF TESTS IN LIEU OF ORIGINAL TEST
CERTIFICATES`; opening "It is hereby certified that original Test Certificate contain the following information in respect of the
material used in the manufacture of the boiler or components thereof bearing Makers Number … according to Drawing Number …";
closing "This satisfies the requirements of Indian Boiler Regulations, 2026."; sign-off Maker's Representative / Maker, Competent
Person / Inspecting Authority. Columns: component, quantity, size, cast/heat no., plate no., steel making process, specification,
steel maker/part maker, certificate no. & date, heat treatment, chemical analysis, yield, UTS, elongation, bend, inspecting authority.

**Form XXII** (was XVII): `[Regulation 555(2)]`; 12 numbered items (maker, manufactured for, location, identification, drawing no.,
design pressure, size L/W/H/Dia, material specification table for shell/furnace/tube plates/flange and tubes/pipes/pads with
chemistry and mechanical properties, volumetric capacity, heating surface, nozzle connections a–e, shop hydro test pressure + date);
certification "as per Indian Boiler Regulations, 2026".

## What the app does (updated 2026-10-17)
- Each QC document has an **IBR version** (1950 or 2026), chosen by QC on New document and in Edit details. New documents start on
  2026; documents made before this stay 1950 and reprint exactly as before.
- Under 2026 the folder prints separate pages built line by line from the gazette's blank forms: Form I(Part-B), IV(Part-A),
  IV(Part-B), V(Part-B), IV(Part-G), IV(Part-D), XXII. The 1950 pages are untouched.
- 2026 form sets: boilers I(B) + IV(A) + IV(B) + V(B); SIB XXII + IV(B) + V(B); PRS IV(D) + V(B); headers IV(G) + V(B).
- Section-by-section detail, data sources and open points for an outside review: `docs/ibr-2026-folder-review.md`.
