# IBR 1950 folder — proposed changes (for approval)

Status: **proposal only, nothing changed.** Approve or reject each item; approved items will be made to the 1950
pages only, and every other 1950 line will stay exactly as it prints today.

Compared on 2026-10-17: our current 1950 folder output vs the official forms in "INDEX for FORMS under the Indian
Boiler Regulations, 1950" (Maharashtra Directorate copy, 84 pages,
https://mahaboiler.in/boiler/images/pdf/IBR_CERTIFICATES&FORMS.pdf). Page numbers below are that PDF's.

**Background.** Our 1950 pages were built from the client's real filed folders (they were accepted by the Telangana
Directorate). Where the client's wording differs from the official blank, we followed the client. So each item
below is a choice: keep the accepted client wording, or switch to the official wording.

Legend: **Fix** = a mistake on our side, recommend changing. **Choice** = client wording vs official wording, your call.
